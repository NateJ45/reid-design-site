What happens after you call.

No guesswork and no pressure. You'll always know where things stand and what happens next.

  1. We walk your space

    Day one · 60 to 90 minutes

    A quick intro call to make sure we're a fit, then I come over and we talk through what isn't working.

  2. You get a plan

    Two to three weeks

    Mood board, layout, furniture and decor picks, and a sourcing list. You see everything before anything is bought.

  3. We shop

    At your pace

    Buy from the list yourself, or I source and order it for you. Trade vendor access either way.

  4. The reveal

    The last visit

    Everything placed, styled and accessorised. Then I step back and you walk in.
