My Story

Why I Started Reid Design

I didn't start Reid Design because everything was perfectly lined up. I started it because I've always known how much a home can shape the way you feel. A well-designed space isn't just about how it looks, it's about how it functions, how it flows, and how it supports your everyday life.

Growing up, I watched my grandmother rearrange furniture the way other people solved puzzles, intuitively, patiently, until something clicked. I didn't realize it at the time, but that's where it started for me. I learned early on that a space can feel right, or it can feel off. And that difference matters.

Over time, that instinct turned into something I couldn't ignore. I saw how many homeowners wanted a home that felt warm, cohesive, and truly theirs, but felt overwhelmed by where to begin or discouraged by the process.

So I built Reid Design to be different. Approachable. Thoughtful. Honest.

I'm based in Plainfield and work with clients across Greater Indianapolis who want more than just a pretty space, they want a home that works. I'm transparent about cost, clear about the process, and intentional with every detail so your space feels both elevated and livable.

Because at the end of the day, design isn't about perfection. It's about creating a home that feels like you, comfortable, functional, and lived in.
