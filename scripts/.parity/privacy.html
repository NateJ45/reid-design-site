What I collect

When you fill out the contact form, you share your name, your email address, and whatever you tell me about your space. The form is delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms, a form service. I do not collect anything beyond what you type into it.

The map on my contact page is from Google Maps. When it loads, Google may set its own cookies, which are covered by Google's privacy policy.

What I do with it

I use what you send to reply to you and, if we work together, to plan your project. I do not add you to a mailing list. That is it.

What I do not do

I do not sell your information, share what you type into the form with anyone outside the form service that delivers it, or run ad retargeting of any kind. There is no Facebook Pixel and no LinkedIn tag. How visits to this site are measured is explained below.

Deleting your information

If you want me to delete anything you have shared, email me at staci@reiddesignllc.com and I will take care of it.

How traffic is measured

This site runs no analytics. Visits are not counted, and nothing is stored on your device for measurement.
