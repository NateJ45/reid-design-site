Pricing & Cost

3questions

  • Costs vary by scope. At Reid Design: an in-home consultation is $225 for a 60 to 90 minute session. Full Room Design starts at $995 and includes a complete design plan with mood direction, layout, furniture and decor selections, and a sourcing list with direct shopping links. Signature Room Design + Styling starts at $1,795 and adds hands-on shopping, paint color selections, and an in-home styling and final reveal. E-Design starts at $695 if you are outside the area or prefer to install things yourself. Hourly Shopping and Sourcing is $100 per hour, no minimum. Whole-home projects are quoted after the consultation, starting at $2,500. The Indianapolis market generally runs $50 to $200 per hour for hourly work, with full room packages from $1,000 to $5,000.

  • No. The design fee is stated upfront before any work begins. The only additional cost is the furnishings and materials you choose to purchase, and you're always in control of that budget. For full-service clients, trade vendor markups are disclosed transparently at the start. For homes outside the local area, a small travel fee may apply, always quoted upfront before booking.

  • For larger full-service projects, fees are structured in milestones, a deposit at signing, a second payment at concept presentation, and a final payment at installation. For smaller packages like consultations and Shopping & Sourcing hours, full payment is required upfront.

Logistics

5questions

  • The consultation is a single 60 to 90 minute visit. The design plan typically takes 2 to 3 weeks to build after the consultation. From there, shopping and styling timing depends on scope and on furniture lead times, anywhere from a few weeks for in-stock items to several months for custom pieces. Hourly Shopping & Sourcing turnaround depends on what you need. I'll always give you a realistic estimate, not a best-case scenario.

  • For full-service clients where I manage purchasing, I handle all claims, returns, and reorders. Vendor relationships and trade accounts come with better resolution processes than retail. I deal with it so you don't have to.

  • For full-service projects involving renovation, I coordinate with contractors but don't act as a general contractor. I can provide referrals to trusted tradespeople in the Indianapolis area and manage design-side communication.

  • I have accounts with vendors not open to the public. These lines offer more distinctive pieces, and my trade pricing is passed along to you, frequently offsetting a portion of the design fee.

  • My primary focus is residential, but I'm open to small commercial spaces, boutiques, offices, short-term rentals. Reach out and describe your project.
